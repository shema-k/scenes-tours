About us · Our story

A youth-led travel company from Kampala

Scenes Tours & Travels is a youth-led, registered Ugandan tour company based in Kampala, dedicated to making travel more accessible, affordable and memorable. Through quality services and cost-friendly packages, we connect travelers to breathtaking destinations while shaping the future of tourism.

Our people

Company structure and leadership

Scenes Tours & Travels is led by the Managing Director, supported by the Deputy Managing Director, with four departments that plan, finance, secure and promote every trip. Department heads report to the Deputy Managing Director.
